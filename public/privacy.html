Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what ADAMTOOL (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website and its tools, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in, we collect your email address. We email you a sign-in link and code, so there is no password. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (see section 4).
  • Usage records. When you are signed in and open or use a tool, we record which tool, the kind of action (for example opening or closing it), the time, a visit identifier, your account identifier and your IP address.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider processes standard connection data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the website and keep it secure.
  • A sign-in cookie. We set one essential cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.
  • What you give to a tool. Many tools run in your browser, so what you type, speak or load into them stays on your device. Some tools send your input (for example a drawing file you upload) to a processing service so that it can produce a result. We use that input only to produce the result you asked for.
  • Messages. If you email us, we keep your message and email address so that we can reply.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to let you sign in; to run, secure and maintain the website and tools; to understand which tools are used so that we can improve them; to prevent abuse; to reply to you; and to meet legal obligations. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you asked for, our legitimate interest in running and improving it safely, legal obligations, and your consent where it is needed.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show advertising or build advertising profiles.

4. Who else handles data

We use service providers, and some services are contacted directly by your browser. They have their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase provides sign-in and our database.
  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website and provides network security.
  • A cloud hosting service (Render) runs the processing for tools that convert uploaded files.
  • Google Fonts, unpkg and jsDelivr supply fonts and open-source code. When your browser loads them, they can see your IP address and browser details.
  • Hugging Face: the Wordhue tool downloads its speech model directly from huggingface.co to your browser, so that service can see your IP address.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the security of the service.

5. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including countries whose data-protection laws differ from yours.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account details and usage records until you ask us to delete them or until we no longer need them. We do not currently apply a fixed expiry period. Server and hosting logs are kept by our providers under their own schedules.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your personal data, to receive a copy of it, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us at lakar.team@gmail.com. We will reply within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days. If you ask us to delete your data, we will delete your account and delete or anonymise the usage records linked to you. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections and access controls on our systems. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

9. Children

ADAMTOOL is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If you keep using ADAMTOOL after a change, you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: lakar.team@gmail.com.
