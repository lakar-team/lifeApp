Wordhue · color-coded Japanese → English (beta)

Every word is colored by its role and linked across four lines — Japanese with furigana, romaji, a word-for-word gloss in Japanese order, and the rearranged English. Hover a word to see it everywhere. Faded italic words don't exist in the Japanese; they were added so the English reads naturally.

Rule-based pipeline: GiNZA/SudachiPy parse → JMdict gloss → recursive clause reordering. No generative AI writes the English.
